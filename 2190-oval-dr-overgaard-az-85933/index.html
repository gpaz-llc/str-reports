📋 Methodology

Analysis Assumptions & Methodology

🎯 Comp Selection Criteria
  • • 2-bedroom, 1-bath cabins in the Heber-Overgaard area
  • • Sleeps 5 to 6 guests, matching this cabin
  • • A full 12 months of booking data from AirROI
  • • Established listings rated 4.76 to 4.99 stars
📊 Data Sources
  • • AirROI: Property-level STR revenue, ADR, and occupancy
  • • Airbnb: Live listing data and guest reviews
  • • Comp scoring: each candidate rated on layout, location, guest capacity, amenities, reviews, and data reliability
⚠️ Key Assumptions
  • • Available all 365 nights (adjust for owner stays)
  • • Professional management and guest communication
  • • Professional photos and a well-written listing
  • • Nightly rates that adjust daily to demand
  • • Consistent cleaning and five-star guest care
💡 Performance Drivers
  • • Summer: June through August is the busiest, highest-rate season
  • • Slow months: January through April, where pricing and pet-friendly bookings matter most
  • • Cozy features: wood stove, deck, and fire ring help the listing stand out
  • • Reviews: early five-star reviews move a new listing up in search results
⚖️ Important Disclaimer
This analysis estimates nightly rent (booked nights × average nightly rate) based on comparable property performance. Actual results will vary based on operational execution, market conditions, seasonality, and property-specific factors. Estimates are before Airbnb/Vrbo host fees and all operating expenses, including platform commissions, utilities, property taxes, HOA/strata fees, maintenance, supplies, insurance, and management fees.
